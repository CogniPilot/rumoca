# Torn read-cache follow-up (read-only)

Source at `d5d701be0` confirms a real guarded→primary read-map thrash. `SolveRuntime::algebraic_newton_caches` owns one `SparseNewtonCache` per block; it contains one `TornNewtonCache` with a single `reads: Option<TornReads>`. `AlgebraicProjectionBlock::tearing_candidates()` orders guarded before primary. The affine loop tries both through the same block cache. `TornNewtonCache::solve_scaled` replaces `reads` whenever the value-derived `AffineEliminationLayout` differs; `TornReads::prepare` clones the layout, copies guards, gathers every row coordinate, then calls `JacobianReadMap::prepare`, whose compact lookup invokes `JacobianValueLayout::locate` for every coordinate. For Fourbar's 616 block the prior 6fb census recorded 9,191 guarded pivot declines followed by 9,191 primary successes. On that alternating path, both candidates reprepare on every projection invocation, even though the guarded decline occurs before `TornSystem` binding and leaves the primary numeric factor available. The prior counts are not a fresh counter in the d5d capture.

The single cached `TornSystem` also compares its cloned layout by full derived `PartialEq` on each successful bind; likewise `reads.layout != *layout` performs value comparison on each entry. Thus “preparation-only” work is effectively hot. Current capture shows 2.54% self in `JacobianValueLayout::locate`, 0.60% in an offset-collection symbol, and 11.36% in `TornNewtonCache::solve_scaled`. `__memcmp` is 5.08% self but has no reliable caller in this capture, so assigning it to layout equality would overstate attribution. Neither the entire torn cost nor a speedup is proven to come from read-map preparation.

Narrow general direction for the owner: retain per-block ownership but cache prepared reads separately for construction-issued guarded and primary candidates, bound to the exact immutable layout/application owner; reprepare on owner/source mismatch. Avoid full value-layout equality on repeat entry once that owner is verified. Leave fresh raw zero guards, complete scaled-pivot checks, coefficient-bit comparison, factor revocation, numerical order, certificates and errors unchanged. Keep numeric factor policy independent while testing the read-map change; avoid dense or other n² scratch. A focused alternating-candidate test can use the existing `read_preparation_count()` to prove two warmup preparations followed by reuse, plus foreign-owner/reentry rejection. Recheck current-run attribution after such a change before targeting `memcmp` or remaining torn work. No builds or profiles were run here.
