## Why integration is not complete

1. No new dependency-closed accepted translator stack was adopted. Several
   individually accepted candidates depend on older unresolved repairs.
2. Full production Lean still needs reviewed NonZero numeric-payload semantics,
   collection-storage correspondence and iterator interfaces. Removing an
   emitted opaque function does not discharge all of these obligations.
3. NonZero.get's source body reaches a generic Transmute that Aeneas rejects;
   retaining the range-carrying type also exposes unsupported TPattern.
4. Known declaration/closure, finite-demand refusal and retention issues remain
   recorded. Fresh closure-source evidence improves a narrow paired fixture,
   but is not permission to waive older failures or support stale wire schemas.
5. Charon14 and an older Aeneas reader do not share a schema. The separate
   paired reader candidate is unfinished integration, not a compatibility adapter.
6. UD07 still requires actual DAE projection, the complete catalog/map checker,
   theorem composition, source-fault controls, independent review and replay gates.

The candidate directories and receipts are the detailed source of truth. An
older README may say review requested before a later channel verdict; the saved
channel records that history. Do not infer stack-wide acceptance from a packet.
